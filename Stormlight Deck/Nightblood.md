### Nightblood

Legindary Equipment

Unique

Equiped creature gains deathtouch and s,s,X: Remove nightblood from this creature.

Whenever equipped creature deals combat damage to a player, that player loses one third of their starting health, rounded down.

Equiped creature may always be blocked.

At the start of combat deal X damage to equiped creatrue where X is the number of corruption counters on equiped creature.

At the begining of your endstep equiped creature gains 2 corruption counter.

(Corruption counters are reduced by during your untab step)

If a creature is killed, any creature that has delt damage to the equiped creature this turn may equip this for free. If mulitple creatures attempt to equip this a random one does.
